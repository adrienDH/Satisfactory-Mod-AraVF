# FICSIT AI French language

**ADA speaks French.** This mod gives the FICSIT AI — called **ARA** in the French version of the game — a full French voice-over, based on the game's official French translation.

> 🇫🇷 *ARA parle enfin français !* Ce mod donne une voix française à l'IA FICSIT, à partir de la traduction française officielle du jeu. Version française de cette description plus bas.

## What is voiced

- **All ADA messages** (630): onboarding, tiers 1 to 9, M.A.M. research, hard drives, Project Assembly, space elevator, deaths, building comments, creatures, weather, the AWESOME Shop, the Portable Waste Network, FICSMAS…
- **Alien communications** (55 messages): ADA keeps her voice, the aliens get their own processed choir voice.
- **The drop-pod intro cinematic** and **the launch cinematic** at the end of Project Assembly.

Subtitles keep the official French text and stay in sync with the voice.

## Language option

**Options > Audio > "Langue de l'IA FICSIT"** (FICSIT AI language): **Français** or **English**.
The change applies immediately, even mid-game (on ADA's next message). The option label is translated in the 22 languages of the game.

## Requirements

- Satisfactory 1.2 (build 502094 or later), Epic Games or Steam.
- SML 3.12 (installed automatically by the Satisfactory Mod Manager).
- Client-side only: you can join multiplayer games where other players don't have the mod. Not needed on dedicated servers.

## Known limitations

- The voice is **synthesized** (text-to-speech, see *AI Usage Transparency* below), not recorded by a voice actor.
- Intro cinematic: the game mixes ADA's English voice and the drop-pod ambience in the same audio track. To avoid redistributing any game audio, the mod mutes that track in French mode and plays the French voice with a recreated drop-pod rumble (synthesized noise that follows the original's intensity) over the game's sound-effects track. It is close to, but not identical to, the original ambience.
- The voice always addresses the player as *pionnier* (masculine). The official French subtitles use *pionnière* (feminine) in about fifteen lines; those subtitles are left unchanged.

## Credits and licenses

- **Text**: official French localization of Satisfactory, © Coffee Stain Studios. No game audio file is included in this mod.
- **Voice synthesis**: [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) by hexgrad — Apache License 2.0.
- **French voice (ff_siwis)**: trained on the SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database, licensed CC BY (Creative Commons Attribution) as listed in [Kokoro's VOICES.md](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M/blob/main/VOICES.md). Yamagishi, Junichi; Honnet, Pierre-Edouard; Garner, Philip; Lazaridis, Alexandros (2017). *The SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database*, University of Edinburgh, The Centre for Speech Technology Research. https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/1705
- Audio packaged with **Audiokinetic Wwise**.
- Built with the Satisfactory Mod Loader and the satisfactorymodding toolchain.

## AI Usage Transparency

- **Intro ambience**: synthesized noise shaped by the loudness curve of the original ambience; the analysis used an AI source-separation tool (Demucs) locally, no game audio is included.
- **Voices (sound assets)**: generated with an AI text-to-speech model (Kokoro-82M, French voice *ff_siwis*), then processed with audio effects (ffmpeg) to sound like the FICSIT AI. The alien voice is the same model with a choir effect.
- **Code and tooling**: the mod's C++ code, the voice-generation pipeline and the Wwise/Unreal build scripts were written with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic).
- **Mod icon**: generated by a script written with the help of an AI assistant.
- The French text itself is **not** AI-generated: it is the game's official translation.

# 🇫🇷 Version française

**ARA parle français.** Ce mod donne une voix française complète à l'IA FICSIT (ARA, ADA en anglais), à partir de la traduction française officielle du jeu.

**Ce qui est doublé** : tous les messages d'ARA (630), les 55 communications avec les aliens (avec une voix alien distincte), la cinématique d'arrivée en capsule et la cinématique de décollage du Projet Assemblage. Les sous-titres restent ceux de la traduction officielle, synchronisés avec la voix.

**Réglage** : Options > Audio > « Langue de l'IA FICSIT » : Français ou English. Le changement s'applique tout de suite, même en pleine partie.

**Limites connues** : la voix est une voix de synthèse (IA, voir plus haut). Pendant l'intro en français, le grondement de la capsule est recréé (bruit synthétique qui suit l'intensité de l'original) : le jeu le mélange à la voix anglaise dans la même piste, et le mod n'embarque aucun son du jeu. La voix s'adresse toujours au joueur au masculin (« pionnier »), alors qu'une quinzaine de sous-titres officiels disent « pionnière ».

**Prérequis** : Satisfactory 1.2 (Epic ou Steam), SML 3.12 (installé par le Satisfactory Mod Manager). Mod côté client uniquement.
